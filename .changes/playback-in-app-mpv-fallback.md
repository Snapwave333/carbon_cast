Streams the browser player can't decode (MPEG-2 video, AC-3 audio, unusual
containers) now keep playing inside the app: playback automatically switches
to the built-in MPV engine for that stream instead of asking you to open an
external player. The next channel starts back on your preferred player, and
the external-player suggestions remain for setups without the engine.
