The player visualizer has been rebuilt to look like something worth watching.
Each blob glows in its own colour, they sit at different depths so the cluster
has real space in it, and the light comes from inside them. They also behave
like they know about each other now — one holds the centre, two circle it, one
keeps its distance, one chases whatever is nearest, one bolts on the beat.
