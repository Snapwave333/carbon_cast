The TV guide now clicks through to playback: a programme airing right now
offers "Watch live" in its details dialog and switches the player to that
channel. Guide cells, list rows, search results and dialogs show artwork and
"S2 E13" badges from your EPG feed; programmes without artwork fall back to
the channel logo, and logos or posters render uncropped instead of badly
zoomed.
