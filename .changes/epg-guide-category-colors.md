The TV guide is now colour-coded by genre: movies, news, sports, kids,
music, documentaries and series each get their own accent colour from your
EPG data — a coloured edge and subtle wash on guide cells and timeline
blocks, a colour dot in the list view and search results, and a tinted
category label in the programme dialog. Unrecognised genres get a stable
colour of their own.
